# Visual Review Notes

The pre-delivery review confirmed that the site follows the Technical Editorial direction: deep Zulubing Ink, paper-white content planes, left-aligned headlines, mono labels, section numbering, and restrained Signal Teal. The strongest moments are the homepage hero and the service detail architecture section.

The accepted refinement pass made the data rail a dominant system across page heroes, section labels, process lists, and capability cards. Generic outline geometry was replaced by vertical signal notation with nodes and mono metadata. Service cards now read as dossier panels with explicit numbering and capability metadata. Public language was revised so unconfirmed content is framed as capability examples or discovery-dependent details rather than raw placeholder language.

Responsive screenshots at 390px show that the mobile header, hamburger control, hero typography, form flow, and single-column content remain legible and usable. Desktop screenshots show the intended asymmetric split layouts, connected card rails, architecture visual, and large enterprise CTA/footer system.
